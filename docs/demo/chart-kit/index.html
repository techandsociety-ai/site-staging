TikTok use drops with every decade of age

Nearly three in four Americans 18 to 20 say they use TikTok. Past 70, fewer than one in five do.

Source: CHIP50 survey, Wave 38, fielded May 14 to July 5, 2026; 31,836 U.S. adults, weighted to the adult population.
